ABOUT CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT GROUP

PROFESSIONAL SOLUTIONS.

Creative Development Group is a Cape Town-based general trading and consulting solutions company founded in 2018.

We provide practical services to residential, commercial, private and public-sector clients. Our goal is to make it easier for clients to access dependable solutions through one trusted business.

From maintenance and plumbing to transportation, cleaning, landscaping, supply and marketing, our approach is built around reliability, professionalism and long-term client relationships.
